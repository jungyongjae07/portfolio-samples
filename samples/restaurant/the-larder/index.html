The idea

Cooked to what showed up at the market, not a fixed menu.

We buy first, then write the menu — so what's on the table this week depends on what came in from three farms within an hour of here. Some things stay (the bread, the butter), most things rotate.

Seating is first-come at the counter or booked ahead for a table. We hold four tables back each night for walk-ins.
