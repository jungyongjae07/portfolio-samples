Reissued styles

We design for the fortieth wear, not the first photo.

Arlo Studio releases one small collection at a time — usually under twenty styles — and holds each one until it's genuinely worn out before replacing it. Fabrics are chosen for how they age: wool that softens, cotton that keeps its shape, hardware that doesn't pit.

Every piece is pattern-tested on real bodies before it ships, sized honestly rather than vanity-sized, and priced to reflect a garment meant to outlast a season. Nothing is restocked until the last one sells — the edit you see is the edit that exists.
